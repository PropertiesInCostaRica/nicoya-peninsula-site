Furnished · Pool · Gated golf community

Effortless Costa Rican living

Designed for comfort, this fully furnished two bedroom, two bathroom home offers a warm atmosphere with generous indoor and outdoor spaces.

An extra large living area is filled with natural light from oversized windows, with custom TV cabinetry, ceiling fans and modern lighting. The dining area features wooden pantry cabinets that add warmth and character.

Bedrooms

A private retreat

The primary bedroom has a Guanacaste king size bedframe, abundant storage and an upgraded ensuite bathroom. The second bedroom has two single beds, and the second bathroom has an updated corner shower.

Indoor outdoor living

An outdoor kitchen for eight

The floorplan extends into a spacious outdoor kitchen with extensive cabinetry, an electric stovetop, a stainless steel refrigerator and a serving bar. The outdoor dining area seats up to eight guests.

Pool and gazebo

Low maintenance and relaxing

The fully tiled backyard centers on a private fiberglass pool with a pump less than a year old. Solar lights lead to a raised gazebo, perfect for reading or afternoon naps.
