Two residences · Ocean views · Plunge pool

An extraordinary ocean setting

An arrival to remember, and a character entirely its own.

Set within the Tango Mar community on Costa Rica's Nicoya Peninsula, Casa León del Mar offers the rare balance every buyer hopes for: a secluded, private retreat that is still close to everything. Step through your private gate and, just 110 steps later, you are on the beach. Few homes in Tango Mar offer this kind of direct access.

The gate opens onto tall, elegant palms and lush greenery. Beyond them lies the true reward: a sweeping view of the ocean that takes your breath away from the very first moment.

Ocean views, beautifully kept grounds and the flexibility of two separate residences come together in a peaceful setting, with the community's golf and tennis at your doorstep.

The main home

Built for living indoors and out

Built with exceptional quality and attention to detail, the home has stood the test of time beautifully, and the original architectural plans are available for the new owner. Offered furnished, the two bedroom, two bathroom main home reflects the warmth of traditional Costa Rican design, with terracotta tile floors, soft beige tones and a European style kitchen with granite countertops.

Soaring 7 meter (23 foot) ceilings give the living spaces a remarkable sense of volume and light, while oversized windows and wide patio doors open them to the gardens and the sea.

Bedrooms

Privacy at each end of the home

The two bedrooms sit at opposite ends of the home, each with its own en suite bathroom. The spacious primary suite has two sets of patio doors: one opening onto the rear porch, the other stepping directly out to the private plunge pool.

Plunge pool and porch

Cooled by the ocean breeze

Surrounded by greenery and cooled by the ocean breeze, the plunge pool is the perfect spot for a morning dip or a quiet sunset moment, all in complete privacy.

The ocean facing porch is the natural heart of the home. Its orientation keeps the sun in the right place, and constant breezes keep it comfortable all year for long dinners, quiet afternoons and relaxed evenings.

Playa Quizales

Your private path to the sand

A short walk through your private gate leads down to Playa Quizales, one of the most beautiful and peaceful beaches on the Nicoya Peninsula. Its long stretch of sand faces the calm waters of the Gulf of Nicoya, framed by palms and tropical greenery. No crowds, only the sound of the waves and unforgettable sunsets.

Life with nature

Mornings begin with birdsong

Pheasants roam the gardens, ducks gather at the pond and chickens provide fresh eggs each morning. These resident animals are included with the sale. The trees around the home welcome monkeys, parrots, toucans and iguanas every day.

Guest house and room to grow

A second residence on the property

A separate one bedroom residence, 158 m2 (1,701 sq ft), has its own kitchen, bathroom, garage and carport. It offers independent space for family, guests or live in staff, as well as potential rental income.

The property also allows for additional construction, so a future owner can expand the main home, add guest suites or create new outdoor living spaces. It is offered with a clean title and its original plans.
