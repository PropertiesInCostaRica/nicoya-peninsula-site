An established hospitality opportunity

Hotel Carmen Coral combines flexible guest accommodations, tropical outdoor amenities, communal spaces and excellent transportation access in Playa Tambor.

Accommodations

12 rooms for every kind of guest

Six junior suites with a queen bed, sofa bed and private balcony or terrace. Two large family rooms that sleep up to eight. Three standard rooms with a queen bed. One group room for up to 10 guests, ideal for cycling groups, retreats and tours.

Renovations

Extensively upgraded

Recent work covers the roof and trusses, pool and jacuzzi, bathrooms and plumbing, electrical systems, air conditioning, exterior walls, drainage, lighting and landscaping, supported by a municipal construction permit and professional plans.
