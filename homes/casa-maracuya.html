Two residences · Fruit garden · No HOA fees

Tropical living with real flexibility

Casa Maracuyá brings together tropical living and practical flexibility in Villas del Mar, Tambor. Make your home in one residence, welcome guests or renters in the other, and pick fresh fruit from your own garden.

Set on a quiet side street with few neighbors, the fenced property has two separate, fully furnished residences, a private plunge pool, mature fruit trees and a backyard bordering the Tango Mar Golf Course.

Some photos have been decorated using AI. All furnishings are as seen in the photos.

The bungalow

Warm, natural finishes

The three bedroom main home pairs block construction with custom wood finishes, including tall ceilings made with wood from the land itself. Every bedroom has custom closets and a built in desk, and the modern kitchen has wood cabinetry and a central island.

Outdoor living

Pool, shower and covered dining

The plunge pool with a waterfall invites you to cool off after a beach walk. An outdoor shower and a covered dining area make outdoor living part of the daily routine.

The apartment

Separate one bedroom with a boat friendly garage

Near the entrance, a one bedroom apartment sits above a garage long enough for a 22 foot boat and a car. It has a compact kitchen, an office or den, air conditioning and a Juliet balcony overlooking the yard.

The garden

Fresh harvests from your own yard

The fenced lot has producing citrus, bananas, plantains, dragon fruit and more. Municipal water, electricity and fiber optic internet are in place, with no HOA fees and no rental restrictions. Both homes are offered fully furnished.
