Ocean view · Pool · Split A/C · Good internet · Fully furnished

The whole bay in front of you

Whale Bay Villa sits in the quiet community of Villas del Mar, close to the Tango Mar Resort, with open water all the way to the horizon. Some days you can even watch whales pass through the bay from the pool deck.

The main house has two bedrooms and two and a half baths, and a separate casita with its own bedroom, bathroom and kitchen welcomes family, friends or renters. It is sold fully furnished, ready as a home or an investment.

Some photos have been decorated using AI. All furnishings are as seen in the photos.

The terrace

Life under a wood ceiling

The covered terrace has an outdoor dining table, a lounge, ceiling fans and a hammock, all facing the sea. Large sliding doors open it to the living room with its vaulted, beamed ceiling.

The pool

A pool with ocean views and a poolside pavilion

The pool runs along the front of the property with a sun deck and a covered pavilion, perfect for long afternoons and sunsets over the gulf.

Inside

Furnished and ready to enjoy

The kitchen has granite counters, wood cabinets and stainless steel appliances. Bedrooms are bright and quiet, the bathrooms have stone look tile and walk in showers, and everything comes fully furnished. The rooms have split unit air conditioning, and the house has good internet.

Casita and garage

Room for guests and projects

The casita has its own bedroom, bathroom and kitchen. A large garage with tools doubles as a workshop or work space, and the house and casita each have their own electricity meter.

The grounds and the area

Fruit trees and five minutes to the beach

The fenced 1,117 m2 lot has tropical gardens, stone paths and fruit trees: noni, water apple and a miniature coconut already bearing, plus young avocado and mamon chino. The beach is about five minutes by car, and Montezuma and Santa Teresa are close for dining.
