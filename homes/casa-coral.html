Home  /  Homes for sale  /  Casa Coral

Furnished · Private pool · Small gated community

Indoor outdoor living near the beach

Casa Coral is a beautifully finished three bedroom home in Villas del Mar, where modern construction and a relaxed tropical setting come together minutes from the beaches of the southern Nicoya Peninsula.

Only one year old and built with concrete and steel, it offers about 220 m2 of living space on a generous 750 m2 lot. It is sold fully furnished as shown and was recently launched on Airbnb.

Living spaces

Bright and open

The kitchen, dining and lounge areas share one welcoming space, and large sliding glass doors open straight onto the pool terrace. The kitchen has a stainless steel refrigerator, a 6 burner propane stove and a raised breakfast counter.

Bedrooms

Three comfortable rooms

The primary suite fits a king size bed, opens directly to the pool terrace and has an ensuite bathroom with a tall glass shower. Two more bedrooms have large windows, their own air conditioning and ceiling fans.

Pool and garden

Room to make it your own

The 6 x 4 metre pool with an adjustable waterfall is surrounded by a large tiled terrace. The generous lot leaves space for more landscaping, fruit trees or a garden, and there is already a thriving lemon tree.

  • Separate laundry room with utility sink
  • Dedicated pool equipment room

Location

Beaches, nature and services

Cocolito Beach, its waterfall and hiking trails are within walking distance. Tango Mar is a favorite lunch spot, and Cóbano has supermarkets, banks and shops.

  • 15 minutes to Cóbano Airport
  • 25 minutes to Montezuma and Santa Teresa
  • 40 minutes to the ferry
