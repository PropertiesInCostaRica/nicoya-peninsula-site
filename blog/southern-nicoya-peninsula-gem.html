Why the Southern Nicoya Peninsula Is Costa Rica's Rising Star

Home  /  Blog  /  Rising Star

Ask anyone who has spent a week between Paquera and Santa Teresa and you will hear the same thing: this end of the Nicoya Peninsula feels like the Costa Rica people dream about. What has changed is that more and more people are finding it.

A coast that grew up gently

Over the last two decades, surf camps turned into small hotels, family sodas became restaurants with a following, and villages like Montezuma, Mal País, Cóbano and Tambor added schools, clinics and services. Even so, building is low, the forest still meets the beach and the pace stays unhurried.

The area has its share of famous fans. Gisele Bündchen keeps a home in Santa Teresa and calls it her home away from home, and on the northern side of the peninsula Mel Gibson once held a large estate at Playa Barrigona. They came for the same thing everyone else does: space, privacy and nature.

Longevity is part of the culture

The Nicoya Peninsula is one of only five Blue Zones on the planet, places known for an exceptional number of healthy people in their 90s and beyond. Home cooking, walking everywhere, close families and a strong sense of community shape daily life. Our guide Living in a Blue Zone explains more.

Getting here is simple now

The trip that once took most of a day is now easy. Ferries run from Puntarenas to Paquera and to Naranjo throughout the day, the main roads are better, and small planes fly into Cóbano Airport, so a morning departure from San José can end with lunch by the sea.

A beach for every day of the week

Swim in the calm water of Tambor Bay or Playa Pochote. Walk the long, quiet sand of Playa Quizales in Tango Mar. Take a boat to Isla Tortuga, hike to the Montezuma waterfalls, or watch the surfers and the sunset in Santa Teresa and Mal País. Curú and Playa Quesera add wildlife and turquoise water.

Prices that still leave room

Buying here costs far less than on the busier northern beaches. Today our listings include land near Paquera from US$89,000, homes in Paquera from around US$119,000, and ocean view estates in Tambor and Tango Mar. Rental income from visitors helps many owners cover their costs.

Living the real Costa Rica

Here you buy fish from the boats in Tambor, dance at the town fiestas and greet your neighbors by name. It is pura vida in its original form, close to the roots of the country.

The southern Nicoya Peninsula is a gem being discovered, and we would love to show it to you.
