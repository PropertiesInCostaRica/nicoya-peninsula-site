The Southern Nicoya Peninsula: A Costa Rican Gem Being Discovered

Home  /  Blog  /  A Costa Rican Gem

For years the southern Nicoya Peninsula was the corner of Costa Rica that only surfers and nature lovers knew about. Today it is one of the most talked about places in the country, yet it still keeps the small town, barefoot lifestyle that made people fall in love with it in the first place.

From hidden corner to sought after address

Towns like Santa Teresa, Mal País, Montezuma, Cóbano, Tambor and Paquera have grown steadily, with new restaurants, yoga studios, boutique hotels, schools and services. The growth has come at a human scale: there are no high rise towers, and the jungle still reaches the sand.

That mix has caught the eye of well known names. Supermodel Gisele Bündchen has a home in Santa Teresa and has described it as her "home away from home," according to The Tico Times. Further north on the same peninsula, actor and director Mel Gibson owned a large beachside estate at Playa Barrigona, as The Tico Times reported. When people who can live anywhere choose the Nicoya Peninsula, it says a lot about its beauty and peace.

Life in a Blue Zone

The Nicoya Peninsula is one of the five Blue Zones of the world, regions where an unusual number of people live long, healthy lives. Fresh food, daily movement, strong family ties and a sense of purpose are part of everyday life here, and many newcomers say they feel the difference within weeks. You can read more in our article Living in a Blue Zone.

Easier to reach than ever

Getting here used to be part of the adventure. Today the Paquera and Naranjo ferries cross the Gulf of Nicoya from Puntarenas many times a day, the roads have improved, and short domestic flights land at Cóbano Airport. From San José you can be on the beach the same morning.

Beaches for every mood

Few places offer so much variety in such a short drive. Tambor Bay and Playa Pochote have calm water for swimming and paddling. Playa Quizales in Tango Mar has a long, quiet stretch of sand. Playa Quesera and Curú are framed by forest and full of wildlife, and Isla Tortuga is a short boat ride away. Santa Teresa and Mal País have long surf beaches and some of the best sunsets in the country, while Montezuma adds waterfalls and a bohemian village feel.

Real estate that still makes sense

Compared with Guanacaste's busier coast, prices here are still within reach. On our list today you will find building lots near Paquera from US$89,000, homes in Paquera from about US$119,000, and ocean view estates in Tambor and Tango Mar for buyers who want more space and privacy. Many owners also rent their homes to visitors part of the year.

The roots of Costa Rica

What makes this area special is the people. Fishermen still bring in the catch in Tambor, families gather for the local fiestas, and the "pura vida" spirit is not a slogan but a way of life. Living here means slowing down, knowing your neighbors and enjoying Costa Rica the way Costa Ricans do.

The southern Nicoya Peninsula is a gem being discovered. If you would like to see it for yourself, we are happy to plan a day of visits for you.
