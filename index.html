Living on the peninsula

Quiet beach towns, close to everything

The southern Nicoya Peninsula runs from the ferry town of Paquera, past the sheltered bay of Tambor and the hills of Tango Mar, to Montezuma and Santa Teresa. You get the quiet of small beach towns with San José a short flight or ferry ride away.

Calm water in Tambor

Bahía Ballena is protected from big swell, so its beaches are good for swimming, paddling and kayaking most of the year.

Easy to reach

Short domestic flights land at Tambor's airstrip, and the Paquera ferry connects you by car to Puntarenas and the Central Valley.

Golf and nature

Play a round in Tambor, then explore Curú Wildlife Refuge, the Montezuma waterfalls or the Cabo Blanco reserve.

Value in Paquera

Near the ferry, Paquera offers everyday services and some of the most affordable homes on the coast, many already earning rental income.
