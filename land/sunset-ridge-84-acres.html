Development land · Ocean views · Mixed use

A rare large parcel with sweeping views

This 84 acre property follows two ridges, about 400 and 800 metres long, both on public road. From the top you look out over Playa Carrillo, Playa Sámara and the southern coast of the Nicoya Peninsula all the way toward Santa Teresa, with sunsets that are hard to leave.

Topography has already been surveyed, interior roads exist and an earlier plan laid out 47 lots, so a new owner starts with real groundwork done.

Ready to build

Water, power and flexible use

There is a registered well and two natural streams that run most of the year. Power lines and a transformer are already on the property. The zoning allows both tourism projects such as a hotel and residential homes.

  • Plenty of flat building areas
  • About 4.5 km to the beach
  • About 2 hours from Liberia Airport

Nature

Forest, birds and monkeys

Lush forest covers much of the land, with wild mango and cashew trees, tropical birds and monkeys. It is a natural fit for an eco resort or a community for remote workers.
