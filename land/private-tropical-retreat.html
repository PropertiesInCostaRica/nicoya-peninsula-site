Your own corner of tropical forest

Behind a gated entrance and a rock paved private road, eight hectares of rolling land open up between grassy clearings, shady woods and creeks running over stone. Cóbano is the doorway to the surf and beaches of Santa Teresa, Malpaís and Montezuma.

At the heart of the land stands a round two story cabin. Downstairs is a wide covered patio under hardwood beams, with space for dining and hammocks, an open kitchen and a bathroom. Upstairs, a large bedroom with polished wood floors and wooden shutters looks out over the treetops in every direction.

The opportunity

Room for whatever you dream up
